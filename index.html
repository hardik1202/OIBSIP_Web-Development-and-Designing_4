Career Inspiration

Your career is a marathon, not a sprint. Every line of code you write, every bug you struggle to fix, and every new concept you learn is a stepping stone toward greatness. Don't be afraid of failing, because in the tech world, failure is just your code telling you exactly how to get it right the next time.
